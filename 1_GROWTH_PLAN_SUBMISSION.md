## Slide 2: Marketing Channels & Reconciled Budget Strategy

### Why Zero Paid Ads?
At average tech-skilling Cost-Per-Click in India (₹15–₹25), ₹2,000 yields only 80–120 clicks, resulting in barely 25–35 signups. Paid ads mathematically fail.

### Reconciled ₹2,000 Budget Allocation
```
Total Budget: ₹2,000
├── ₹1,200 → 12 Campus Representatives (CRs) (Tiered: ₹50 at 15 regs, ₹100 at 25+ verified regs)
└── ₹800   → Top 2 Student Referrer Prize Pool (1st: ₹500 Amazon Voucher, 2nd: ₹300 Voucher)
```
*(Note: Milestone rewards for referring 2 peers unlock the digital "Top 50 AI Interview Questions & ATS Resume Pack" at ₹0 marginal cost, completely eliminating budget overruns).*

### Channel Mix (Total: 500 Registrations):
1. **Channel 1: Campus Placement WhatsApp Networks via 12 CRs (Target: 340 Regs | 68%)**
   - **Sourcing 12 CRs:** Tapped within 24 hours from NxtWave's existing 50,000+ student Discord/WhatsApp community, college coding club heads, and past hackathon participants.
   - Each CR receives a unique tracked referral code (`?ref=CR_CODE`) and broadcasts pre-formatted, value-first messages in their official batch groups during prime hours (8:30 PM). Average target: ~28 regs per CR.
2. **Channel 2: Peer-Led LinkedIn & Tech Groups (Target: 35 Regs | 7%)**
   - High-yield value posts highlighting 2026–2027 recruiter expectations shared in student Telegram/Discord placement prep communities.
3. **Channel 3: Built-in Viral Referral Loop (Target: 125 Regs | 25%)**
   - Post-registration referral mechanism ($k = 0.33$): Every registrant receives a custom invite link. Referring 2 peers unlocks the curated interview question pack, driving organic peer invitations.

## Slide 5: Strategic Reflections & Decision Making

### 1. What Changed Between First Idea and Final Solution?
* *Initial Idea:* Run paid Instagram lead ads + build a standard Google Form.
* *Why it was killed:* A ₹2,000 ad budget exhausts in ~100 clicks, delivering fewer than 35 leads.
* *Pivot:* 100% reallocation into **incentivized campus peer distribution (12 CRs)** and a **gamified viral referral pool (top 2 referrers)**, delivering 500 registrations at ₹4.00 CAC.

### 2. If You Had Another 24 Hours, What Would You Prioritize?
* **Primary Focus:** Deploy an **Automated WhatsApp Webhook Bot (via Twilio/Gupshup)** to deliver calendar `.ics` invites and 3 daily micro-prep notes. In ed-tech webinars, industry attendance averages ~45%; automated WhatsApp calendar sync increases the live Show-Up Rate to >70%.

### 3. What Did AI Suggest That Was Deliberately Rejected?
* **Rejected Cold Scraping College TPOs:** Institutional approvals take 2–4 weeks through college committees. On a 7-day clock, institutional outreach has near-zero velocity.
* **Rejected Instagram Meme Page Promotions:** Meme audiences consume content for entertainment, showing poor conversion intent for 60-minute technical builds.
* **Rejected Overly Complex ML Stacks (PyTorch/Transformers from scratch):** Advanced tensor math creates high cognitive friction for Tier-2/3 beginners. Grounding the workshop in practical API/Streamlit tools makes 60-minute deployment realistic and achievable.
