NxtWave Growth Intern – Growth Challenge

Round 1 | Submission Window: 48 Hours

We want to understand how you think, learn, build, take ownership and use AI to solve problems.

You can use ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Lovable, Cursor, n8n, Zapier or any other tools.

We are not testing which tools you know. We are testing what you can do with them.

The Challenge

NxtWave plans to launch a free online workshop:

“Build Your First AI Project in 60 Minutes”

Your mission:

Create a plan to get 500 final-year engineering students to register.

Assume:

You are given the below time and resources : 

Budget: ₹2,000
Campaign Duration: 7 Days 
AI Tools: Anything you want

This is only a simulation. You do not need to contact students or execute the campaign.

What You Need to Do

1. Understand the Student

Tell us:

Who exactly are you targeting?

Why would they care?

What would make them register?

2. Create the Marketing campaign Plan

Show us:

Which channels you would use( Registrations through whats app group , emails , clubs etc)

What you would do

Why it should work

How the 500 registrations could come in

Prioritise. Don't give us 20 ideas.

3. Build One Thing

Create one working asset that supports your plan.

It could be a:

Landing page ( Note : Everyone might build a landing page )

WhatsApp flow

Referral code and tracker 

Building engagement tools during the workshop 

Automation of AI project evaluation

No limits to build with AI 

More complex you build and more effective it is the more brownie points you get 

You decide what is worth building.

4. Show Us How You Thought

Answer these three questions:

What changed between your first idea and final solution?

If you had another 24 hours, what would you improve?

What did AI suggest that you rejected and why?

What You Need to Submit

1. Growth Plan
Maximum 5 slides / 2 pages

2. Working Asset
Live link / prototype / demo

3. AI + Learning Notes
2–3 examples of:
What you asked → What AI suggested → What you changed

4. 3-Minute Video
Explain your approach, what you built and why.

What We Are Looking For

Learnability
Can you learn and improve quickly?

Ownership
Can you take an unclear problem and move it forward?

Intent to Grow
Do you genuinely want to become better?

Bias to Ship
Can you turn an idea into something real?

Problem Solving & Judgment
Can you make good decisions, even with AI available?
