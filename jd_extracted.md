Growth Intern – AI, Experiments & Community

Location: Hyderabad , Work from Office
Internship: 6 Months | Stipend: ₹25,000/month
PPO: Upto ₹7 LPA, based on performance

What you'll do

You will build, launch and test real growth ideas with real users.

One week, you may build an AI-powered website. Another week, you may run student workshops, create a WhatsApp flow, work with creators or analyse why users are not registering for the event .

Your goal is simple: test fast, understand what works and scale the winners.

Build with AI: Use tools like ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Lovable, Cursor, n8n and Zapier to research, build and automate faster.

Run growth campaigns: Work on bootcamps, hackathons, campus programs, communities, creator campaigns and on-ground activations.

Work with numbers: Track registrations, conversions, drop-offs and campaign performance.

Scale what works: Turn successful experiments into repeatable systems that can reach thousands of users.

Work across teams: Collaborate with Product, Tech, Content and Marketing to take ideas from concept to launch.

The way we work:
Idea → Build → Launch → Measure → Learn → Scale

Who should apply?

We are looking for builders, not just resumes.

You may have:

Built an app, website or AI project

Run a college club, community or event

Organised a fest, hackathon or campaign

Built a content page, newsletter or side project

Experimented with AI tools

Started something on your own, even if it didn't fully work

You will fit well if you learn quickly, enjoy solving unclear problems, think creatively, are comfortable with numbers, prefer shipping over overthinking, and believe in speed over perfection.

Small projects are fine. Real attempts matter.

What you'll walk away with

4–6 real projects you can showcase

Hands-on experience with AI, growth and automation tools

Experience working on campaigns with real users and real numbers

Exposure to Product, Tech and Marketing teams

A strong story to take into future interviews

PPO opportunity for a full-time Growth Associate role
