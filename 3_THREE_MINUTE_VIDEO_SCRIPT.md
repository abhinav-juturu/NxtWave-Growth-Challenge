## Spoken Script

### [0:00 – 0:45] The Student Problem & Reconciled Channel Math
*(Visual: Facecam transitioning to Slide 1 & Slide 2)*

> "Hello everyone. Here is my strategic solution to register 500 final-year engineering students for NxtWave's 'Build Your First AI Project in 60 Minutes' workshop on a ₹2,000 budget.
> 
> Current final-year students in the Class of 2027 are facing placement anxiety. Recruiters easily filter out generic academic projects like calculators and library managers. Students need practical, verifiable proof-of-work.
> 
> With just ₹2,000, paid ads fail mathematically: at average CPCs, you run out of money after thirty registrations. 
> 
> Instead, I allocated 100% of the budget into direct campus incentives: ₹1,200 rewards 12 Campus Representatives across target engineering colleges with tiered vouchers, and ₹800 funds Amazon vouchers for the top two student referrers. 
> 
> Reaching 4,200 students across college WhatsApp groups at an 18% click-through and 49% direct conversion delivers 375 signups. A 33% viral referral lift brings in another 125, reaching exactly 500 verified students at just ₹4 per registration."

Instead of overwhelming beginners with complex enterprise infrastructure, I structured four realistic tracks, such as a Streamlit Document QA Assistant and a Job Matcher. Students see the build pipeline, the code, and an ATS resume template with guidance on measuring their own results.

> Now let's register. When a student enters their batch details and submits...

*(Visual: Facecam with final slide)*

>

> 
> If I had another 24 hours, I would deploy an automated WhatsApp webhook to deliver calendar invites and pre-workshop notes, boosting live attendance from the industry average of 45% to over 70%.
> 
> This project embodies NxtWave's growth loop: Idea, Build, Launch, Measure, Learn, and Scale. I am excited to bring this speed and ownership to NxtWave as your Growth Intern. Thank you!"
