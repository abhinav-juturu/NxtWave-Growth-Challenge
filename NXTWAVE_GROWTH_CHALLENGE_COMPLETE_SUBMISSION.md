## 1. Executive Summary & Strategy

When approaching this challenge, we rejected generic approaches (basic HTML landing pages and paid ads) in favor of **first-principles growth engineering**:

1. **Student Psychology First:** Final-year B.Tech students in Tier-2/3 colleges (Class of 2027) are experiencing intense placement anxiety during the 2026–2027 recruitment season. Recruiters are easily filtering out dated classroom assignments like "Library Management Systems". What students need is an **authentic, working project to list on their resume and GitHub** that will help them clear placement interviews.
2. **Channel Focus & Virality over Paid Ads:** With ₹2,000, paid Meta/Google ads yield only ~25–35 registrations before budget exhaustion. Instead, we allocate 100% of the ₹2,000 into **Campus Representative (CR) incentives (₹1,200)** and a **Top 2 Referrer Prize Pool (₹800)**, achieving 500 registrations at a blended CAC of just **₹4.00**.
3. **High-Value Interactive Asset:** Rather than a static landing page, we engineered the **AI Project Studio & Verified Admit Pass**—an interactive web application where students can preview projects across 4 beginner-friendly career tracks, view live build pipelines, generate honest ATS resume bullets with measurement placeholders, claim a digital student badge, and unlock milestone resources through peer referrals.

## 2. Deliverable 1: 5-Slide Growth Plan

### Slide 1: Target Persona & Core Positioning
* **Target Audience:** Class of 2027 (7th/8th Semester) B.Tech / B.E. students (CSE, IT, ECE, AI/DS) primarily in Tier-2 and Tier-3 engineering colleges (JNTU, Anna University, VTU, AKTU, RTU affiliates).
* **The Reality:** 2026–2027 placement season is active. IT service company hiring is selective; product companies demand practical, modern AI/Full-Stack capabilities.
* **The Pain Point:** Students suffer from "Resume Imposter Syndrome"—their resumes list generic academic assignments that get flagged by recruiter screening.
* **The Hook:** *"Don't just watch a webinar. In 60 minutes, code a working AI application, deploy a public URL, and format honest ATS resume bullet points that demonstrate verifiable proof-of-work."*
* **Credibility Factor:** Free, browser-based, zero GPU required, backed by NxtWave (WEF Technology Pioneer 2024, NSDC Partner).

### Slide 2: Marketing Channels & Reconciled Budget Strategy
* **Budget Strategy:** ₹2,000 allocated 100% into high-trust peer distribution:
  * **₹1,200** $\rightarrow$ 12 Campus Representatives (CRs) in target colleges (Tiered: ₹50 at 15 regs, ₹100 at 25+ verified registrations).
  * **₹800** $\rightarrow$ Top 2 Student Referrer Prize Pool (1st: ₹500 Amazon Voucher, 2nd: ₹300 Voucher).
  * *(Digital interview packs unlock at 2 referrals with ₹0 marginal cost).*
* **Channel 1: Placement WhatsApp Groups via 12 CRs (Target: 340 Regs | 68%):** Outreach through 12 student coordinators broadcasting pre-formatted, value-first messages in official batch groups during prime hours (8:30 PM).
* **Channel 2: Peer-Led LinkedIn & Tech Groups (Target: 35 Regs | 7%):** High-yield placement guide summaries dropped into student Telegram/Discord placement prep communities.
* **Channel 3: Built-in Viral Referral Loop (Target: 125 Regs | 25%):** Registrants unlock the *"Top 50 AI Interview Questions Guide"* by referring 2 peers ($k = 0.33$).

### Slide 3: 7-Day Timeline, Scenarios & Measurement
* **Day 1:** Deploy interactive customizer + parameter tracking (`?ref=`). Test registration flow. *(Cumulative: 15 pilot tests)*
* **Day 2:** Onboard 12 Campus Representatives across target colleges with tracked invite links. *(Cumulative: 65)*
* **Day 3:** Core batch blast across official placement WhatsApp groups at 8:30 PM. *(Cumulative: 185)*
* **Day 4:** Referral leaderboard launch; viral loop kicks into high gear. *(Cumulative: 290)*
* **Day 5:** 30-second screen-recording teaser showing the live AI app running. *(Cumulative: 380)*
* **Day 6:** Scarcity & cohort final call before server sandbox provisioning closes. *(Cumulative: 450)*
* **Day 7:** Morning countdown reminder + final push to cross 500. *(Cumulative: 500+)*

**Mathematical Funnel Model (Base Case):**
$$\text{Reach (4,200 across 30 batch groups)} \xrightarrow{\text{18% CTR}} \text{Visitors (756)} \xrightarrow{\text{49.6% CVR}} \text{Direct Regs (375)} \xrightarrow{+33\%\text{ Viral Lift}} \mathbf{500\text{ Registrations}}$$
* **Effective Blended CAC:** $\frac{₹2,000}{500} = \mathbf{₹4.00}$ per student.
* **Contingency Rule:** If below 150 registrations by Day 3, trigger internal NxtWave student community broadcast (zero marginal cost) to boost momentum.

### Slide 4: The Working Asset — Beyond a Basic Landing Page
* **Placement Diagnostic Assessment:** 3-question interactive check allowing students to benchmark their current project portfolio against recruiter expectations.
* **Beginner-Friendly 60-Minute Customizer:** 4 realistic tracks (*Streamlit Document QA Assistant, Job Matcher, Browser Focus Telemetry, Code Reviewer*). Features build pipelines, accessible tech stacks, and honest ATS resume bullet templates with bracketed `[X]` metrics.
* **Personalized Digital Admit Pass:** Generates a verified student badge with unique Ticket ID, candidate name, college, selected track, and join instructions.
* **Transparent Peer Referral Hub:** Dynamic invite URLs (`?ref=TICKET_ID`) with 1-click WhatsApp sharing, automated local deduplication, and a sample benchmark ambassador leaderboard.

### Slide 5: Strategic Reflections & Decision Making
* **What Changed:** Shifted 100% of budget from paid social ads to **incentivized campus peer distribution (12 CRs) and a gamified referral pool**. Grounded the asset in beginner-friendly, accessible 60-minute tools.
* **If Given Another 24 Hours:** Deploy an **Automated WhatsApp Webhook Bot (via Twilio/Gupshup)** to deliver calendar `.ics` invites and 3 daily micro-prep notes, boosting live attendance from the industry baseline of 45% to **>70%**.
* **What AI Suggested That Was Deliberately Rejected:**
  1. *Scraping and cold emailing College TPOs:* Bureaucratic approvals take 2–4 weeks; zero velocity on a 7-day clock.
  2. *Paying Instagram meme pages:* Low intent and poor conversion for serious technical workshops.
  3. *Overly complex ML stacks (Pinecone/LangGraph from scratch):* Creates high cognitive friction; freshers need practical, beginner-friendly tools that deploy in 60 minutes.
